REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Cloud Migration and Infrastructure Services

ORGANIZATION: Global Healthcare Systems
RFP NUMBER: GHS-CLOUD-2024
DEADLINE: April 30, 2024

INTRODUCTION

Global Healthcare Systems is seeking qualified vendors to provide cloud migration and infrastructure management services. We currently operate on-premises data centers and wish to migrate to a cloud-first strategy while maintaining compliance with healthcare regulations.

REQUIREMENTS AND QUESTIONS

1. Cloud Migration Experience

   1.1 What is your experience with large-scale cloud migrations in the healthcare industry?

   1.2 Describe your migration methodology and typical timeline for enterprise migrations.

   1.3 How do you minimize downtime during migration?

2. Healthcare Compliance

   2.1 How do you ensure HIPAA compliance in cloud environments?

   2.2 What experience do you have with HITRUST certification?

   2.3 Describe your approach to protecting Protected Health Information (PHI) in the cloud.

3. Technical Expertise

   3.1 What cloud platforms do you recommend for healthcare workloads and why?

   3.2 How do you handle hybrid cloud architectures?

   3.3 Describe your approach to infrastructure as code and automation.

4. Disaster Recovery

   4.1 What disaster recovery solutions do you implement for healthcare systems?

   4.2 What are your typical RPO and RTO capabilities?

   4.3 How do you test disaster recovery plans?

5. Ongoing Support

   5.1 What managed services do you provide post-migration?

   5.2 How do you handle 24/7 support for critical healthcare systems?

   5.3 What tools do you use for monitoring and alerting?

6. Cost and Timeline

   6.1 Provide a rough estimate for migrating 500 servers and 2 PB of data.

   6.2 What cost optimization strategies do you recommend?

   6.3 How do you ensure projects stay on budget?
